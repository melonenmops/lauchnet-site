About CaseFiles

CaseFiles is an automated content publishing service that creates and distributes true-crime storytelling videos on TikTok. Using AI-assisted tools, the service produces narrated, dramatized case stories and publishes them to a single, operator-owned TikTok account. The service operates exclusively through the official TikTok API and accesses only the operator's own account.

Content Notice

Stories presented on this channel are fictional or dramatically interpreted accounts inspired by real events. They are produced for entertainment purposes only and do not constitute journalism, legal commentary, or factual reporting. Names, details, and narratives may be altered or composited. Viewer discretion is advised.
